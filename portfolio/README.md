# Professional Proof of Work

This portfolio shows how I approach work across four connected lanes: marketing and consumer trust, regulatory response operations, AI product risk, and regulated growth.

The materials are intentionally practical. They are designed to show how I translate business goals, regulatory obligations, product decisions, technical constraints, and customer impact into workflows, controls, evidence, escalation paths, and measurable execution.

All examples are public, fictional, or generalized. They do not disclose confidential employer or client information and are not legal advice.

## Portfolio Areas

### [Marketing & Consumer Trust](marketing-consumer-trust/README.md)
Campaign review, claims and disclosures, partner marketing, digital communications, launch governance, and consumer-risk review.

### [Regulatory Response Operations](regulatory-response-operations/README.md)
Regulatory intake, legal-to-technical translation, evidence requests, deadline tracking, escalation, response readiness, and post-matter learning.

### [Regulated Growth Strategy](regulated-growth-strategy/README.md)
Customer journeys, growth experiments, partner strategy, product-to-market translation, commercialization, and scaling in regulated environments.

### [Operational AI Governance](https://github.com/ashminibasant/operational-ai-governance)
Ownership, evidence, human oversight, escalation, evaluation, control design, and auditability for AI-enabled systems.

## How to Read This Portfolio

Each section contains reusable frameworks and templates rather than generic commentary. The goal is to make my decision-making visible: what I would ask, what I would document, what I would measure, when I would escalate, and what I would require before calling a process ready.
