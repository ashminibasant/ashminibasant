I am a systems thinker, operator, and founder. Most of my career has been spent walking into complicated environments, figuring out what is actually broken, and building the structure needed to make the work safer, clearer, faster, and scalable.

My background crosses financial services, compliance, operations, revenue, consulting, nonprofit leadership, and emerging AI governance. I am strongest in the space between strategy and execution, especially when there is risk, regulation, multiple stakeholders, and no clean playbook.

Across all of this work, I keep coming back to the same questions. What happens when the system meets the real world? Who owns the outcome? What controls exist? What evidence is retained? What happens when something fails? What can the organization prove later?

### Status Unknown

I also founded **Status Unknown**, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit focused on people living with chronic, invisible, complex, disabling, and undiagnosed conditions.

Status Unknown grew from the belief that visibility alone is not enough. People need real support, resources, navigation, community, accessibility, and systems that actually account for the realities of disability and chronic illness.

The work includes visibility, direct relief, resource navigation, community connection, accessibility, workplace rights, disability barriers, and ADA related education and advocacy. The broader vision also includes stronger legal referral pathways, employment support, public accountability, policy advocacy, and systems change.

Status Unknown has distributed more than **$20,000 in direct community assistance** while continuing to build the infrastructure for a larger national support and advocacy ecosystem.
